40+ Years of Trust
SM Shahi Masala

From a Family Kitchen
to Every Kitchen in India

It began in 1982 in a small home in the heart of Uttar Pradesh, where our founder Shri Shyam Mohan ji ground spices by hand each morning using recipes passed down from his grandmother. What started as a labour of love for the neighbourhood soon grew into something far greater.

Today, SM Shahi Masala reaches thousands of households — yet our process remains the same. We still select whole spices by hand, slow-roast them the traditional way, and grind them fresh. No shortcuts. No compromises. Just the same honest flavour your family has trusted for generations.
